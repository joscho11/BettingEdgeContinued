## 7. What this does not prove

- **That he declines.** Zero repeats in a 7-season club is a pattern in a small sample, not a mechanism.
- **That the touchdowns crash to 5.8.** That is a 9-season mean, and one of the nine held.
- **That the separation drop is aging.** One year, one measure, no route or coverage context behind it.
- **That 46 passing touchdowns fall to 32.** The 15-team mean describes the group, not this team.
- **A 2026 guarantee.** This is a 2025 profile, a set of historical base rates, and a live price.
