## 7. What this does not prove

- **That the Campbell usage survives LaPorta's return.** The overlap is real. The 17% projection is the hedge, not a claim that 19.4% share repeats.
- **That six targets are guaranteed.** TeSlaa, Petzing's TE usage, and St. Brown's volume can all eat into that.
- **That 12 drops were a fluke.** Last of 48 is the flag. A 6-target week with another drop cluster is week 4 all over again (8 targets, 4.7 half-PPR in 2025).
- **Charted yards per route.** These are on-field pass snaps.
- **A PFF grade.** None appear. Public sources only.
