# Who's the best manager in your league?

*2026 · how to load your Sleeper league on the League History page*

**The one-liner:** Paste your Sleeper league ID, hit Load, and the page walks every linked season. Six tabs. Insights first. The numbers on screen in the video are one worked example, not a ranking of your league.

---

## 1. How to open it

On the site: **More → League History**. The ID is the long number in your league URL: `sleeper.com/leagues/{ID}/league`.

First load walks standings, drafts, and weekly scores for each linked year. A 3-year league is usually about 10-20 seconds. The same ID is instant for an hour after that. The page works on a phone. It is easier to read on a computer.

---

## 2. The six tabs, in order

- **Draft & Roster Insights** (opens first). Three views: My Team, Best Values, Draft Room. My Team is historical lineup production after four rostered weeks. Best Values covers cheap hits, $5+ FAAB that scored (and $5+ that scored zero), and trades graded from the week after the deal. Draft Room is how the league drafts, year to year.
- **All-Time Leaderboard.** Headline cards, then win rate versus scoring against that week's league average, then ranked win rate.
- **Hall of Fame.** Eight record cards (high score, painful loss, blowout, closest, low score, luckiest win, highest and lowest combined games). Chaos Map under them plots every game by total points and margin. Luckiest Win is all-play, not the lowest winning score.
- **Rivalries.** Build a Week, Explore a Matchup, League Matrix. Build a Week returns one full-week slate in Classic, Maximum Drama, or Fresh Blood.
- **Report Cards.** One manager at a time: winning, scoring versus the weekly league average, and consistency as a plus-or-minus point swing around that manager's own average. All-time is season by season. One year goes weekly.
- **Consistency & Luck.** Steadiest, most volatile, most fortunate, most unfortunate. Scatter is scoring versus week-to-week swing, steadier at the top. Bars are actual wins minus how often that week's score would have beaten the rest of the league.

---

## 3. What this does not prove

- **Who is the best manager in your league.** The page shows the record. It does not pick a winner for you.
- **That a one-year 10-0 is an all-time win-rate crown.** All Time win % needs 2 seasons.
- **Playoff destiny from Draft Room heatmaps.** Those are draft tendencies, not results.
