# Garrett Wilson Fantasy Outlook 2026

*2026 · WR, New York Jets*

## 7. What this does not prove

- **That Wilson is a bad receiver.** Three 1,000-yard seasons on mediocre quarterbacking.
- **That Geno caps him at WR18.** Three seasons and three different WR1s is a small base, and New York is not Seattle.
- **That 2025 tells us anything.** Seven games, two knee windows.
- **That ESPN is wrong.** If you think the Jets throw more than Seattle did, WR15 is the price you pay.
