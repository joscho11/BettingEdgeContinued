# Justin Jefferson's Fantasy Outlook 2026

*2026 · WR, Minnesota Vikings*

**The one-liner:** Justin Jefferson went from WR2 ADP to WR25 in half-PPR in 2025. The video's read is that the drop sits mostly with the quarterback, not a collapse in Jefferson himself. WR6 in 2026 is already a heavy price. The call is a better 2026 than 2025, not a smash over that ADP.

Live board as of 2026-08-17: Sleeper ADP **WR6** (11.0), Draft Board Model Proj **WR7** (181.0 half-PPR), Sleeper published projection **WR9** (205.4). Those three numbers are the board. The take in the video is mine.

The path in the video is modest: if Murray is an average NFL quarterback, or even slightly below, that is already a different room than McCarthy 2025.

Murray's number-one pass catcher, yards per route on Murray throws only, min 150 routes, 2019-2024. It is not a cap on Jefferson and it is not a 2026 projection.

## 7. What this does not prove

- **That Jefferson had no part in 2025.** Receiver metrics held. Catch rate, explosives, and yards before catch still fell on his targets.
- **That Murray 2024 is what Minnesota will get.** Different offense, different weapons, and the 2025 Arizona sample is too small to rank.
- **That WR6 is a buy.** The video says the opposite on price: heavy, hard to beat. The bounce is versus 2025, not versus ADP.
- **Charted yards per route.** These are on-field pass snaps.
- **A PFF grade.** None appear. Public sources only (nflverse, NGS, PFR / Sportradar, PBP).
