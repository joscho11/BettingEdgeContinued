## 7. What this does not prove

- **That 140 targets repeat in Tennessee.** Ridley, Tate, Ayomanor, and Ward's accuracy all eat looks.
- **That Daboll will keep the Kafka aDOT.** 2024 last-of-57 is the play-caller he actually was with this receiver. 2025 W1-10 already being deep does not make Daboll the 2025 play caller.
- **That 8.0 tgt/g with Nabers equals 2025.** Same season target total. Different per-game rates.
- **That Ward's late TD/INT split is a leap.** YPA stayed 5.75. aDOT got shorter.
- **A WR16 repeat.** The projection is 9.9 PPG if he plays.
- **That WR47 is a smash.** The video uses no lock language. The lean is slightly above ADP, not much upside.
- **Charted yards per route.** These are on-field pass snaps.
- **First-down rate as a spoken case.** 2025 42.4% (39/92), 53rd of 56 WR with 40+ catches. Devil's-advocate on the short-area profile. Not in the VO.
