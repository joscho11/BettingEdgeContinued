## 8. What this does not prove

- **That 25.9 PPG is the floor.** That was RB1 of 99 in a 10-game window with Jones, a 1st-in-EPA offense, and a 43.5% opponent slate.
- **That 12.6 PPG is the new mean.** Six games, Jones hurt, 68.6% opponent slate, Rivers at the end.
- **A 17-game 2026.** 2021 and 2025 are the only 17s.
- **That receiving will catch Gibbs / Bijan / CMC.** 0.92 YPRR is the measured gap.
- **Charted yards per route.** Receiving YPRR is PFF. Rush YCO is PFF. Split YAC/A is PFR. RYOE is NGS. They are different series.
- **Independent 253 vs Sleeper 254.** Those points were kept off the cut. Speak ranks.
